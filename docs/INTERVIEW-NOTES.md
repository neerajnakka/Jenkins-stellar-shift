# Interview Notes

Use this file to record what you personally learned from each hands-on exercise.

For every component, answer:

## 1. What?

What problem does it solve?

## 2. Why?

Why is it part of the pipeline?

## 3. How?

How does it work technically?

## 4. Failure

What can fail?

## 5. Troubleshooting

What commands/logs/metrics would you inspect?

## 6. Security

What could be exploited or leaked?

## 7. Scalability

What changes when the engineering organization grows?

## 8. Interview explanation

Explain it in 30-60 seconds using your own words.

Do not claim that this lab was your employer's production pipeline. Use it as practical experience to support technically honest interview answers.
